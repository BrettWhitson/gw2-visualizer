About GW2 Visualizer

An unofficial fansite: a free, non-commercial fan-made tool for exploring Guild Wars 2 crafting trees. Not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored or approved by ArenaNet or NCSOFT. Game content is used under ArenaNet's Content Terms of Use.

Data

  • Items, recipes, currencies, prices and icons: the official Guild Wars 2 API.
  • Mystic Forge recipes: derived from the Guild Wars 2 Wiki via its public query API. Wiki contributor content is available under the GNU Free Documentation License 1.3 (wiki copyrights); game content remains © ArenaNet LLC.

Privacy

No accounts, cookies, analytics or tracking. Your view settings are kept in this browser's local storage and game data is cached in IndexedDB. The app only talks to api.guildwars2.com, render.guildwars2.com and, when you open an item's details, wiki.guildwars2.com (to list vendors and containers; turn this off in Settings).
