Guild Wars 2, visualized

Free tools that run entirely in your browser, built on the official Guild Wars 2 API. No sign-up, no tracking.

  • Crafting Search any item and explore its full crafting tree as a graph: Mystic Forge recipes, trading post costs, buy-or-craft hints, the cheapest path and a shopping list. Open the crafting explorer →

    Try The Bifrost, Aurene's Tail or Perfected Envoy Helmet.

  • What you can craft Everything your account can make right now from what it owns, ranked by value, and a graph of what each material can become, step by step. See what you can craft →

    Needs a key with the inventories permission (add characters and wallet for levels and currencies).

  • Characters Connect an API key to see each character's equipped gear laid out like the hero panel, with rune sets, upgrades, infusions and gear-only attribute totals. Connect your account →

    Needs a key with the characters and builds permissions. It stays in your browser.

  • Engine sandbox Play with Prismatrix, the graph rendering and physics engine behind the crafting graphs: generate graphs of up to 10,000 items, try every layout, force and style, drag, scatter and shake them, and watch the engine's numbers live. Open the sandbox →

    No account needed. Nothing you change here touches your crafting settings.

More visualizers (account inventory, achievements, legendary progress) are on the way.
